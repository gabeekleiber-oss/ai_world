## Intake procedure

1. Confirm the asset's author, exact license, redistribution and modification permissions, and any attribution obligations **on the individual asset page**. Marketplace-wide license claims are insufficient.
2. Record the source URL and license evidence. Retain required attribution in this register and any distribution credits.
3. Verify that texture, mesh, animation, font, audio, and HDRI components are licensed separately when bundled.
4. Prefer CC0, original procedural content, or explicitly permissive licenses compatible with redistribution. Flag share-alike, noncommercial, editorial-only, AI-training restrictions, or unclear ownership for owner review **before use**.
5. Record edits, compression, conversions, and derivative work. Recheck terms before release and if the source license changes.
6. Never commit downloaded third-party assets without an approved entry here.
